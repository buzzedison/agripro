# AgriPro Knowledge Hub Resources

This directory contains various practical resources for farmers and agribusiness professionals:

## Available Resources

1. **ROI Calculator** - Calculate return on investment for crops and livestock
2. **Crop Planning Calendar** - Seasonal planning guide for different crops and regions
3. **Soil Health Assessment Tools** - Guides and calculators for soil testing and management
4. **Water Management Calculator** - Tools for irrigation planning and water conservation
5. **Carbon Footprint Estimator** - Calculate and reduce farm carbon emissions
6. **Market Price Analyzer** - Track and predict agricultural commodity prices
7. **Equipment Cost Calculator** - Evaluate machinery purchase vs. leasing options
8. **Labor Management Tools** - Workforce planning and cost optimization resources
9. **Pest Management Decision Guide** - Economic thresholds and treatment calculators
10. **Grant and Subsidy Finder** - Tools to identify applicable agricultural funding programs
